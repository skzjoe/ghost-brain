# Vendor / Subcontractor Negotiation

Use when the user is negotiating with suppliers, freelancers, subcontractors, service vendors, or implementation partners.

## Issues to map beyond rate

- unit price / rate
- payment timing
- delivery timeline
- quality standard
- warranty / defect handling
- revision rounds
- capacity / volume commitment
- escalation path
- communication cadence
- exclusivity / priority treatment

## Key questions

- Is the vendor optimizing for margin, cash flow, predictability, or workload planning?
- What matters more to them: faster payment, larger volume, clearer scope, or lower service burden?
- What can be standardized to reduce ambiguity and rework?

## Good trade examples

- faster payment ↔ better rate
- clearer scope ↔ stronger delivery commitment
- guaranteed volume ↔ preferred pricing
- fewer revisions ↔ tighter timeline and lower cost

## Watchouts

- vague quality acceptance criteria
- unlimited revisions by accident
- delivery risk without consequences
- overcommitting volume without contractual protection
