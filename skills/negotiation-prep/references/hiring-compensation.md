# Hiring / Compensation Negotiation

Use when the user is preparing for salary, title, role scope, retention, or offer-package discussions.

## Issues to map beyond base salary

- base salary
- bonus / variable pay
- title
- role scope
- reporting line
- flexibility / remote policy
- review cycle
- probation expectations
- learning budget
- growth path
- KPIs / success definition
- equity or long-term incentive if relevant

## Key questions

- Is the candidate optimizing for cash, title, growth, flexibility, or certainty?
- What part of the package matters most to them?
- What can be traded without distorting team equity?
- What would make the role attractive without overspending on fixed compensation?

## Good trade examples

- slightly lower base ↔ faster review cycle
- title clarity ↔ tighter scope boundaries
- flexibility ↔ stronger output expectations
- learning budget ↔ moderated base increase

## Watchouts

- solving every concern with salary alone
- vague role scope that creates future mismatch
- promising progression without a review structure
- losing leverage by revealing internal limits too early
