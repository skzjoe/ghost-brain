# Negotiation Cheat Sheet

## Core principles

- A good deal is one that is better than the BATNA.
- Do not confuse position with interest.
- Explore before exchanging terms.
- Use multiple issues to create room for trade-offs.
- Do not give concessions for free.
- Package offers often reveal the other side's priorities.
- Convert a good conversation into explicit commitment.

## Fast pre-meeting questions

- What do I actually want?
- What happens if this deal does not happen?
- What else can I negotiate besides price?
- What likely matters most to the other side?
- What can I trade without hurting myself?
- What is my walk-away point?
